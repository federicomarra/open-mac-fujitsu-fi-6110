fi-6110 Scanner

Your Fujitsu fi-6110 scanner back at work on your Mac.

Installation

  1. Drag the fi-6110 Scanner app onto the Applications folder (shown next to it).
  2. Connect the scanner to your Mac with the USB cable and turn it on.

First launch (only needed once)

macOS may show a warning because the app doesn't come from the App Store. This is normal:

  1. Open the Applications folder.
  2. Right-click the app (or hold Ctrl and click) and choose Open.
  3. In the window that appears, press Open again.

If the "Open" button doesn't appear: go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom and press Open Anyway.

After that, a normal double-click is all you need.

How to use it

  1. Put the sheets in the scanner's feeder.
  2. Open the app: when the dot at the top right is green, the scanner is ready.
  3. If needed, change the options on the right (kind, resolution, format…) and press Scan.
  4. The file is saved in the folder shown under "Save to" (usually Documents), named after today's date, for example 2026-08-24-scan. You can change the name before scanning.

The app is ready to go: Scan both sides and Skip blank pages are on automatically, so you capture both faces and blank backsides are dropped by themselves.

Your default settings

Want the app to always start with the options you prefer? Open the fi-6110 Scanner → Settings… menu (or press ⌘ ,) and choose the kind, resolution, paper size, both-sides scanning, file format and default folder. From then on the app opens just like that.

Full guide inside the app

In the menu bar, Help → "How to Use fi-6110 Scanner" has a step-by-step tutorial with everything you need. Keep it handy the first few times.

Tips

  • PDF (OCR): as the format, it creates a PDF where you can search and copy the text of the document.
  • Scan both sides: captures both faces of the sheet in a single pass.
  • Rotate pages automatically: if a sheet goes in upside-down, the app notices from the text and turns it the right way up by itself.
  • Skip blank pages: automatically drops blank backsides.
  • Rearrange the pages: after scanning you can drag the pages to change their order, press Reverse order to reverse it, or Flip all to turn every page 180°. The saved file updates by itself.
  • Rotate a page: double-click a page to flip it (180°), or right-click it to rotate right, left, flip it, or delete it (the app asks first).
  • Add to a PDF: press "Add to a PDF" and choose a document you already have: its pages appear in the window (with a PDF badge) and the sheets you scan are added at the end. The original document's pages stay untouched, text included. The result is saved onto the PDF you picked; if you'd rather not replace it, switch off "Overwrite the file if it already exists". With a PDF loaded, "Flip" and "Reverse" act on the newly scanned pages only.
  • Overwrite the file if it already exists: normally, if a file with the same name already exists, the app creates a new one adding -1, -2… so you don't lose the old one. Turn this option on if you'd rather replace it.
  • If the app says the scanner isn't connected: check the USB cable and that the scanner is turned on, then wait a few seconds.
